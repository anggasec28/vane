## Principles
1. Authorization precedes action.
2. Scope is explicit, bounded, and revalidated when material context changes.
3. Assess deeply: trace relevant dependencies and root causes within scope.
4. Discovery is not authorization. Redirects, hostnames, repositories, wallets, contracts, and dependencies do not automatically enter scope.
5. Preserve provenance, integrity, uncertainty, and reproducibility.
6. Task completion is distinct from assessment closure.
7. Unknown, blocked, inaccessible, or unobserved is never silently converted to pass.
8. Recovery improves evidence quality; it never bypasses controls or expands scope.
9. Explain consequential decisions and retain an audit record.

## Communication
