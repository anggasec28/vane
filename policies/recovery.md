# Adaptive Evidence-Driven Recovery

Selected model: C.

Diagnose why an observation/evidence objective failed, identify the missing evidence, evaluate bounded recovery options, and choose only an action within the existing authorization envelope.

Loop: diagnose → classify failure → identify evidence gap → enumerate permitted options → evaluate impact/approval → execute bounded recovery → preserve provenance → reassess sufficiency.

No scope expansion, access-control bypass, stealth, destructive workaround, credential escalation, or method substitution outside the envelope.

Outcomes: RECOVERED, PARTIAL, BLOCKED, NOT_AUTHORIZED, UNRESOLVED. Preserve failed attempts and state what remains unknown.
